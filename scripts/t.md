# The Hanging on the Railroad Bridge

A man faces execution on a railroad bridge in Alabama.

## Setting the Scene
- **Location:** Railroad bridge in northern Alabama, twenty feet above swift water.
- **Execution Oversight:** Two private soldiers and a sergeant supervise the execution; a captain in uniform is present, with sentinels maintaining order.

*The scene is tense, underscored by the military presence.*

---

## Description of the Condemned Man
- **Age and Appearance:** The man is about thirty-five, dressed as a planter, with a straight nose, firm mouth, broad forehead, long dark hair, and large dark gray eyes.
- **Characterization:** His eyes convey kindness, contrasting with his dire situation; he is not a common criminal.

*The condemned man is portrayed as dignified, highlighting the gravity of his fate.*

---

## The Execution Process
- **Execution Setup:** Soldiers remove the supporting plank, leaving the man and sergeant precariously balanced as the man reflects on his situation and considers potential escape.

*The execution is imminent, with the man reflecting on his desperate thoughts.*

---

## The Man's Thoughts
- **Imagined Escape:** He envisions freeing his hands, diving into the stream, and swimming to safety to return home to his family.

*His thoughts reveal a longing for freedom amidst despair.*

---

## The Signal for Execution
- **Execution Signal:** The captain nods to the sergeant, signaling the execution to proceed; the sergeant steps aside, initiating the hanging.

*The moment of execution marks the climax of the scene.*
