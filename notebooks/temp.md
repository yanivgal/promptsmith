## Research Question 1:
**What is the strength and direction of the relationship between faculty's perceptions of the importance of course design, communication, time management, and technical competency, and their ability to teach online?**

- Descriptive statistics (mean and standard deviation) were reported for each of the four competency dimensions: course design, communication, time management, and technical competency (Table 2, p. 23).
- Most items were rated high for both **Importance** and **Ability**.

### Highest Rated Items (Importance):
- **Course Design**:
  - Manage grades online (M = 4.73)
  - Create online assignments (M = 4.68)
- **Course Communication**:
  - Respond to student questions promptly (M = 4.79)
  - Provide feedback on assignments (M = 4.65)
- **Time Management**:
  - Schedule time to design the course prior to delivery (M = 4.65)
  - Spend weekly hours to grade assignments (M = 4.54)
- **Technical Competency**:
  - Complete basic computer operations (M = 4.74)
  - Navigate within LMS (M = 4.67)

### Highest Rated Items (Ability):
- **Course Design**:
  - Manage grades online (M = 4.61)
  - Create online assignments (M = 4.60)
- **Course Communication**:
  - Send announcements/email reminders (M = 4.82)
  - Use email to communicate with learners (M = 4.79)
- **Time Management**:
  - Schedule weekly hours to facilitate course (M = 4.39)
  - Spend weekly hours to grade assignments (M = 4.37)
- **Technical Competency**:
  - Complete basic computer operations (M = 4.64)
  - Navigate within LMS (M = 4.36)

### Key Finding:
- Perceived **Importance** of competencies closely aligns with perceived **Ability** to perform them.
- Weak positive correlation between Importance and Ability:  
  `r(223) = .249, p < .001`

    - Creating online assignments (M = 4.68)
  - **Course Communication Competency:**
    - Responding to student questions promptly (M = 4.79)
    - Providing feedback on assignments (M = 4.65)
  - **Time Management Competency:**
    - Scheduling time to design the course prior to delivery (M = 4.65)
    - Spending weekly hours to grade assignments (M = 4.54)
  - **Technical Competency:**
    - Complete basic computer operations (M = 4.74)

- Findings indicate that competencies in importance for course design and communication were scored the highest.
- **Key finding:** More support is likely needed for faculty in course design and communication competencies.

## What demographic factors are related to faculty's perceptions of the importance of course design, communication, time management, and technical competency, and their ability to teach online?
**Demographic factors influencing faculty perceptions were analyzed.**
- Demographics included:
  - Gender
  - Academic rank
  - Online teaching methods
  - Level taught
- ANOVA results showed:
  - Significant differences in gender and level taught regarding the importance of online teaching competencies.
  - No significant differences in gender, academic rank, and online teaching methods.
  - Significant differences in the level taught based on perceptions of ability to teach online.
- **Key finding:** Demographic factors, particularly level taught, significantly influence perceptions of teaching ability.
